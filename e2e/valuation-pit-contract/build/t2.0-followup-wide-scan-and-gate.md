Trigger (b) does not fire. MPWR is not an anchor, it does not move ±5% (it does not move at all),
and it does not change side of a gate.

**Why it cannot cross under any FCFF value this change can produce** — arithmetic, not extrapolation:

1. While the router status is `Disputed`, `recompute_member` (`valuation_high_signal.rs:509-530`)
   sets `point_estimate_unreliable = true`, `provisional_rates = true` and
   `valuation_unavailable_reason = Some("disputed_candidates")` **unconditionally**. Three of the
   four failures are therefore independent of any FCFF value while the status is `Disputed`.
2. Leaving `Disputed` requires the candidate gap to fall to ≤ 5000 bps. `difference_bps` is
   `|f − c| · 20000 / (f + c)` (`operating_valuation.rs:745`), strictly decreasing in the FCFF
   candidate `c` for `c < f`. Here `c = 54630 < f = 91657`, and the correction is uniformly
   **downward** — all twelve of MPWR's interest years flip positive → negative, and Probe D measured
   every per-year dFCFF as negative. A lower `c` widens the gap. The router moves further into
   `Disputed`, never out of it.
3. Even if the FCFF lane were somehow selected, the street band would still fail, and by more:
   the band needs a base in `[118920, 246988]` cents (0.65…1.35 × street 182954). The forward base
   91657c misses it by 4990 bps; the FCFF candidate 54630c misses it by **7014 bps**. The only way
   to clear the band is a *higher* base, and this change only lowers FCFF.

So the answer holds in both directions of the residual uncertainty below.

## One thing the measurement found that changes the plan's scope

**The sign convention on its own is inert. Measured, not argued.**

The counterfactual rewrote twelve years of MPWR's filed interest in the history
(`2014 +1.1M → −1.1M` … `2025 +29.2M → −29.2M`) and **every downstream number was bit-identical**,
including the model's own internal after-tax interest add-back:

```
interest add-back   Some(85) -> Some(85) bps of revenue
fcff candidate      Some(54630)c -> Some(54630)c
```

Writing `FcfPoint::interest_expense_dollars` directly bypasses the setter's `.abs()`
(`dcf_model.rs:907`), but the two **read** sites re-apply it to whatever the field holds:

* `dcf_model.rs:551` — `let interest = interest.abs();` in the FCFF audit
* `dcf_model.rs:1590` — `let interest = interest.abs();` in the aligned driver bridge

What I measured is therefore **the sign convention with LD-1's read-site removals absent**, and the
measured answer is that in that configuration it changes nothing, anywhere — not MPWR, not the FCFF
candidate, not the add-back margin the model actually consumes.

**Consequence for the plan:** Wave 2b's `negatedQnames` contract change is a no-op unless LD-1's
three `.abs()` removals land *with* it. They are not independent work items and cannot be sequenced
apart; shipping 2b alone would produce a policy fingerprint bump and zero behavioural change, which
is the worst of both — a contract that claims a convention the code does not honour.

**The residual I did not measure, and why.** The full three-site counterfactual cannot be run from a
test: it requires deleting those two `.abs()` calls, which is a production edit. I did not make it.
Two quantities are therefore unmeasured — the FCFF *intrinsic value* under the corrected series, and
whether the forward candidate shifts through its `return_on_capital_bps` / `own_growth_bps`
coupling to `fcff_analysis`. Neither can overturn the gate answer above (point 3 is sufficient on its
own), but if you want them, the enabling change is exactly two lines and I will run it under an
explicit instruction rather than assume one.

## Method notes

* Two passes, because the providers cost differently. Pass one asks SEC alone the sign question over
  all 501 names. Pass two spends a Yahoo classification call and a second SEC document only on the
  48 that answered yes — several hundred classification calls would have measured the provider's
  rate limiter rather than the filings.
* Universe = `fetcher::DEFAULT_LIVE_SYMBOLS` (the S&P 500 the shipped Windows screener actually
  values) ∪ `PROBE_COHORT` ∪ the BAC control. The union deduped to 501: every pinned cohort name is
  already in the shipped universe.
* Absence stays absence. `no history`, `no bridge` and the 5 missing CIKs are printed as such and
  are excluded from the distribution rather than entered as zeros. The one genuine `0 bps` in the
  series (YUM) is a measured zero shift, not a missing value.
* The gate probe fetches forward evidence and market params **once** and shares them across both
  runs, so the only difference between baseline and counterfactual is the interest series.
* Live risk-free was available (463 bps), so market params were not provisional; `provisional_rates`
  fails for MPWR because the `Disputed` branch sets it, not because the rate feed was down.
