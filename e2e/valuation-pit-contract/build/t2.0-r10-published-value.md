## The finding that most changes the shape of the pre-registration

**Nineteen of twenty-five affected issuers move $0.00, and it is not because the change failed to
reach them.** The tree check confirms the counterfactual is live; all 25 have ≥1 rewritten year. The
correction arrives and is *absorbed*, by two distinct mechanisms:

1. **Robust normalization over the series.** The FCFF base is a robust statistic across the whole
   history, so a year or two flipping in a fifteen-year series does not move it. WSM is the clearest
   case: its normalized after-tax interest add-back flips sign outright, `28 → −28` bps, and both its
   FCFF candidate and its published value are unchanged to the cent — the two robust statistics are
   medians over different years.
2. **The router publishes the forward lane.** ROL, TPR, XYZ and others carry an FCFF candidate that
   the correction could move, but the published number is the forward candidate.

So the honest magnitude statement is **not** "25 issuers move by up to 157 bps". It is:

> Of 25 affected issuers, **6 move a published number**, all downward, median **−60 bps**, worst
> **−157 bps** (ROST) / **−357c** (MPWR). **19 move $0.00.** **0 change router lane.** **0 produce a
> non-positive FCFF.** **4 anchors move $0.00.**

Both halves matter to a pre-registration: predicting that 25 names move would be falsified by the
wave just as surely as predicting that none do.

## An instrument defect I found and fixed mid-measurement

My first pass labelled eighteen issuers `BLOCKED`, on the reasoning that an unchanged interest
add-back meant the rewrite never reached the model. That was wrong: the add-back is a *robust*
statistic, so it can legitimately sit still while the rewrite arrives. Reporting those eighteen as
"blocked" would have claimed the counterfactual was not working when in fact it was working and
being absorbed — the opposite conclusion, on the same numbers. The label is gone; the probe now
establishes tree state once, directly from the setter, and reports the add-back and the FCFF
candidate as numbers for the reader to judge. The table above is from the corrected run.
