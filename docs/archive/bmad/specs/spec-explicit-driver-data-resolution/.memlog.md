topic: Explicit financial driver resolution without silent defaults
updated: 2026-07-30T19:37
---

- (direction) Create a canonical contract for Windows and Android driver resolution; defaults must not silently participate in FCFF WACC.
- (capability) CAP-1: resolve aligned annual operating and rate inputs from all eligible sources before valuation or refusal; success is explicit source, period, quality and rejection provenance.
- (capability) CAP-2: keep FinancialServices on residual income and OperatingNonFinancial on FCFF; success is no financial FCFF fallback and explicit unavailable reasons.
- (capability) CAP-3: expose downstream unavailable states without synthetic intrinsic, gap or score; success is UI reason plus analyst-only behavior when analyst data exists.
- (capability) CAP-4: enforce exact Windows/Android parity; success is exact cents, bps, enums, fingerprints and reason codes, with one-cent mutations failing.
- (constraint) FinancialServices never resolves FCFF debt, interest or tax-shield inputs; missing book or ROE returns unavailable.
- (constraint) Missing debt never means zero; reported zero debt is NotApplicable; interest with zero debt is provider inconsistency.
- (constraint) Historical effective tax and marginal WACC tax are separate fields; effective tax is not terminal tax.
- (constraint) Source alignment uses fiscal period, duration, instant date, units, currency and filing provenance rather than asOfDate alone.
- (decision) Cost-of-debt source order is observable market data, rating or synthetic spread, SEC aligned interest over average debt, Yahoo aligned annual data, then unavailable.
- (decision) Evidence quality is solid at three or more valid periods, provisional at one or two real periods, and unavailable after exhaustive source attempts with no valid period.
- (decision) Public parity uses integer cents and bps with no epsilon; floats terminate at the same rounding boundary on both platforms.
- (assumption) The new spec supersedes only conflicting default CoD/tax clauses in historical artifacts; historical files remain unchanged for audit.
- (event) Intent and decisions accepted for implementation on 2026-07-30.
- (implementation) Windows and Android now share explicit annual driver/rate resolution, financial-services residual-income routing, unavailable cache propagation, policy `business-class-policy/7-explicit-driver-resolution`, and exact fixed-point parity.
- (verification) Automated Windows/Android gates pass; parity is exact for 23/23 QA-scope cases and a deliberate one-cent mutation fails. No application was launched; any live QA remains `qa`-only with a maximum of 20 symbols.
