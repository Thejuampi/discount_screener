## Session Overview

**Topic:** Improve signal-to-noise on the Windows app so opening it answers *exactly* what is needed without cognitive overload — and so recommendations go beyond binary “good time to enter” into conditional, timed, zone-aware guidance.

**Goals:**

1. Define the open-app information architecture that maximizes decision SNR.
2. Design a communication density band: denser than tags, lighter than reports.
3. Specify “conditional action intelligence”: stance + reasons for further downside + wait horizon + buy zone $A–$B.
4. Ground ideas in current Windows surface (opportunity list, detail, scoring, regime, advisor, thesis/action_bullets, entry bands).
5. Produce prioritized, implementable directions — not a feature dump.

### Context Guidance

Current product already has pieces of the puzzle:

- Multi-model scoring + presentation (`scoringPresentation*`, DetailPanel).
- Market-context cards with evidence bullets (plan: 4 dimensions wording).
- Advisor actions (add/hold/trim/exit) mapped from setup labels.
- Backend fields that already lean toward nuance: `action_stance`, `thesis_*`, `action_bullets_*`, `entry_low_cents` / `entry_high_cents`.
- Rich secondary panels (TA, regime, quant, news, estimates) that can *cause* noise if all shout at open.

User constraint (explicit): **no extremes** — no walls of text, no three-word empty labels. App should *go further* than “es buen momento” and say things like:

> “Ahora es buen momento, pero por X e Y tiene chances de ceder un poco más; esperaría días/semanas/meses y compraría en zona $A–$B.”

### A. First Principles — What is “signal” at open? (#1–#18)

**[FP #1]: Decision Residue**
_Concept_: On close, the app should leave a mental residue of *what to do next with which names*. Signal = residue that survives after closing the window.
_Novelty_: Measures success by memory, not by screen fullness.

**[FP #2]: Three Verbs Only**
_Concept_: Every open session resolves to Act / Wait / Avoid (plus size trim/exit if portfolio-aware). Everything else is supporting evidence.
_Novelty_: Collapses the action space before designing UI.

**[FP #3]: Time Is Half the Decision**
_Concept_: “Buy” without horizon is incomplete. Signal includes *when* (now / days / weeks / months).
_Novelty_: Elevates time from footnote to co-equal with stance.

**[FP #4]: Price Without Zone Is Vanity**
_Concept_: A single “fair value” is weaker than a buy zone $A–$B with invalidation above/below.
_Novelty_: Zone + invalidation as default, not advanced.

**[FP #5]: Reasons Must Be Falsifiable**
_Concept_: X & Y motives for further drop must be checkable facts (RSI, distance to support, earnings in N days, regime, extension), not vibes.
_Novelty_: Treats narrative as a claim with tests.

**[FP #6]: Confidence Is Not Score**
_Concept_: Score ranks candidates; confidence says how much to trust *this* conditional plan. Separate axes.
_Novelty_: Prevents high score from faking high certainty.

**[FP #7]: Open = Portfolio + Watchlist Delta**
_Concept_: First paint prioritizes *what changed since last open* that alters a decision (new setup, zone hit, thesis broken).
_Novelty_: SNR as delta, not absolute dump.

**[FP #8]: One Primary Name Focus**
_Concept_: Default open can spotlight 1 “decision of the day” + short list of runners-up, not equal volume for 40 tickers.
_Novelty_: Editorial hierarchy like a newspaper front page.

**[FP #9]: Evidence Budget = 3**
_Concept_: Hard cap: at most 3 evidence bullets visible at first level; rest behind progressive disclosure.
_Novelty_: Codifies the 4-dimensions plan’s max-3 as a global law.

**[FP #10]: Secondary Panels Are Libraries, Not Alarms**
_Concept_: TA, news, congress, quant are *on demand*; they do not interrupt the primary decision card.
_Novelty_: Role separation of surfaces.

**[FP #11]: Noise = Unactionable Truth**
_Concept_: True statements that don’t change Act/Wait/Avoid are noise (e.g. “market is open”).
_Novelty_: Actionability filter for every string.

**[FP #12]: Signal = Difference That Makes a Difference**
_Concept_: Bateson-style: only show what would change the user’s plan vs their default.
_Novelty_: Comparative, not absolute, presentation.

**[FP #13]: The Middle Density Sentence**
_Concept_: Canonical form is one compound sentence or 2 short lines: stance + condition + horizon + zone — not a paragraph, not a badge.
_Novelty_: Explicit linguistic unit of design.

**[FP #14]: Model Explains Itself in User Language**
_Concept_: Never show internal factor IDs; always concrete metrics (as in 4-dim plan).
_Novelty_: Aligns with existing editorial work.

**[FP #15]: Wait Is Not Weakness**
_Concept_: UI must not frame Wait as failure or lower-status than Buy; it’s often the high-SNR output.
_Novelty_: Cultural fix for binary trading UIs.

**[FP #16]: Staleness Is Signal**
_Concept_: “Data as of … / last refresh …” is SNR if it prevents acting on dead quotes.
_Novelty_: Meta-signal about trust.

**[FP #17]: Conflict Is Signal**
_Concept_: When value says Buy and timing says Wait, the *conflict* is the message — not an averaged mush.
_Novelty_: Surfaces disagreement instead of hiding it.

**[FP #18]: Progressive Truth**
_Concept_: Level 0: stance+horizon+zone. Level 1: 3 reasons. Level 2: charts/factors. Level 3: raw model.
_Novelty_: Truth depth without first-level overload.

## Appendix — Canonical Sentence Templates (seed)

**Wait (long):**  
`{Name}: buen setup de valor, pero {caution_1} y {caution_2}; esperaría {horizon} y compraría en {zone}.`

**Act now (long):**  
`{Name}: plan de entrada ahora — {support_1}; {support_2}. Zona preferida {zone}; invalidación {invalidation}.`

**Avoid:**  
`{Name}: no actuar — {caution_1}; {caution_2}. Revisar si {invalidation_flip}.`

**Hold / own:**  
`Ya tenés {weight}%: {stance_verb} solo dentro de {zone}; fuera de zona, mantener y no promediar.`

**Short symmetry:**  
`Buen momento para short, pero {rebound_risks}; esperaría {horizon} hacia {zone} para mejor entrada short.`

These are seeds for implementation, not final i18n keys.
