- (change) Core: FR-31 residual income landed, completing the kernel. Financial issuers are valued as book plus the PV of (roe - cost of equity) * book, with the spread fading at the same rate as book growth -- one erosion event, not two, so no second parameter the cross-section cannot fit. Closed form verified against direct numerical integration and against a hand-derived flat-path row (1320.00). Three structural consequences: the debt bridge moved out of publish() into projection::equity_value, so financials no longer have to assert a fabricated net debt of zero; the series summation moved into a private numerics module both models share; and non-positive book plus growth-above-ROE (equity-financed growth this model cannot price) are refusals rather than published numbers. The COF ROE-normalization question (Open Question 5) is untouched and stays a Shell evidence-construction decision -- the Core values whatever return posterior it is handed.
- (change) Shell adapter landed (valuation_core_adapter.rs): builds the evidence bundle from provider data, fits the credit curve and the fade rate cross-sectionally in a first pass, values each issuer in a second. Credit fit uses leverage alone with a zero coverage slope, because leverage*coverage*rate == 1 identically on accounting data and a two-regressor fit recovers that arithmetic rather than a credit market.
