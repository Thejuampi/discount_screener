- (decision) Valuation publishes only when point-in-time evidence supports every material component, corporate overhead, capital bridge, physical basis, terminal reinvestment, and accounting regime.
- (capability) CAP-1: Preserve bitemporal evidence for structured and unstructured sources, including economic period, publication/knowledge timestamp, revision lineage, source vintage, and retrieval metadata.
- (capability) CAP-2: Classify issuers and valuation components point-in-time; permit SOTP components rather than forcing one model family per ticker.
- (decision) SOTP components emit enterprise value only; a single consolidated bridge subtracts net debt, NCI, preferred claims and adds separately evidenced non-consolidated investments.
- (decision) Disclosed unallocated corporate overhead is a separate negative-EV consolidation component; material unreconciled overhead blocks consolidated intrinsic publication.
- (decision) Partial component coverage may show verified covered EV diagnostically but cannot emit an intrinsic price, gap, or valuation score.
- (decision) ResourceProducer models use commodity-specific volumes, prices, costs, hedges, reserve categories, decline and finite reserve lives; no BOE-value shortcut or terminal perpetuity without demonstrated replacement.
- (decision) ContractedInfrastructure is a distinct family requiring explicit take-or-pay, fee/volumetric, or percent-of-proceeds contract evidence.
- (decision) RBL uses an explicit convergent capital-structure policy; non-convergence, multiple fixed points, or unsupported gross/WI/NRI reconciliation rejects valuation.
- (decision) Every terminal perpetuity declares terminal ROIC and reinvestment through g = reinvestment rate times ROIC; finite resources require finite horizons unless replacement is evidenced.
- (decision) SBC is recognized exactly once, either as economic expense or equity dilution; working capital and CapEx require separate provenance.
- (decision) Disputed compares compatible model and analyst intervals only as external diagnostics; analyst consensus, market price, and later returns never enter intrinsic computation or calibration.
- (decision) Backtesting has a primary point-in-time driver-accuracy track and a secondary market-outcome diagnostic track. Historical index membership, delistings, corporate actions, and family/component history are required.
- (decision) 40-F and IFRS issuers require a native IFRS normalizer handling interest classification and IFRS 16. Until then they return SourceRegimeUnsupported without US-GAAP fallback.
- (decision) Valuation quality aggregates hierarchically from driver quality and materiality through component contribution to consolidated confidence; sensitivity and RBL stability are inputs.
- (constraint) No hard intrinsic-to-price caps, analyst calibration, sector haircuts, default royalty burdens, arbitrary partial-coverage penalties, or silent numeric substitutions.
